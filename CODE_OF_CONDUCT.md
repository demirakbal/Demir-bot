# Project conduct — prepared for the private pilot

Use respectful, concrete discussion in project issues, pull requests and any project-run channel. Critique ideas and behavior without personal attacks, harassment, threats, discrimination or unwanted repeated contact. Respect other people's time, privacy, authorship and source licences. Do not publish private messages, credentials or identifying information without applicable permission.

The project owner is the maintainer and handles project participation decisions. A private conduct-reporting/moderation route has not yet been selected; that public moderation detail remains pending. The separately selected security-reporting destination is maintained in SECURITY.md. If you already have a verified private route to the maintainer, ask there for the appropriate channel before sharing sensitive details. Do not guess a contact or put sensitive allegations/security material in a public issue. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.

For an actual report, the maintainer should consider the relevant evidence and affected people, limit access to necessary details, distinguish observed facts from allegations and choose a proportionate project response. A clarification, warning or participation restriction may fit the circumstances; this prepared policy does not create automatic sanctions, a guaranteed investigation timeline or a promise of confidentiality the project cannot enforce. External legal/account actions require their actual authority.

This source policy is prepared for the solo-maintained project; no public channel, reporting inbox, automated moderator or account permission was created by saving it. Review the real reporting route and platform rules before a public launch.
