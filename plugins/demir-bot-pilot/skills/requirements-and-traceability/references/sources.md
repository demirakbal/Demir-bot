# Selection and provenance

Inspected using Firecrawl, 2026-09-21. This is a focused adaptation, not an unchanged upstream package or a claim of extensive upstream testing.

Foundation: [Addy Osmani spec-driven-development](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills/blob/main/skills/spec-driven-development/SKILL.md). MIT copyright 2025 Addy Osmani retained in LICENSE. Reused specification-first intent, capability decomposition when warranted, explicit assumptions/success criteria, existing-system conventions and living specs. Removed compulsory multi-stage human gates for routine authorized work, five-file limits, default commands/thresholds and automatic module-merging advice. Added requirement-level traceability and evidence discipline.

Compared:
- [a5c-ai traceability-matrix-builder](https://github.com/a5c-ai/babysitter/blob/main/library/specializations/domains/business/business-analysis/skills/traceability-matrix-builder/SKILL.md): strong coverage of links, orphans and change impact, but largely a capability outline with assumed process/tool dependencies; universal 100% targets and link presence do not establish execution or satisfaction. Not imported.
- [Agile V requirement-architect](https://github.com/Agile-V/agile_v_skills/blob/main/requirement-architect/SKILL.md) and its README: formal baselines and provenance, but depends on a broader lifecycle, independent roles and repeated gates. CC BY-SA source was evaluated, not incorporated into this MIT adaptation. Its README describes contract tests; those were not executed here, and no compliance claim is adopted.

Demir's previously reviewed requirements/design/testing course materials informed the custom consistency checks: measurable criteria, stable IDs, requirement-to-assertion evidence, current-scope counts and change history. Use actual current files for exact quotations or project decisions. Sources use live main branches and may change.
