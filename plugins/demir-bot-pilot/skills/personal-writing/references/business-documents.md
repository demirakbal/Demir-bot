# Venture and investor document workflow

Use for a requested pitch, business brief, one-pager, proposal or investor-outreach draft for an actual venture. Reuse approved facts, content-repurposing and relevant research; keep email/LinkedIn tone and delivery with their existing owners. A business request does not create a new finance service or permit investor contact.

## Establish the evidence

Identify venture/product, intended reader, document purpose, stage and requested format. Inspect supplied product/customer evidence, actual shipped scope, team contribution, business model assumptions and any confirmed traction. Mark missing facts as unknown or explicit placeholders. Separate paid customers, signups, interviews, pilots, simulated outcomes and aspirations; none substitutes for another. Do not invent revenue, users, partnerships, funding, testimonials, founder biography or investor relationships.

For market/competitor statements, use dated primary sources where possible and the existing research procedure. A broad market estimate does not establish reachable demand; label bottom-up calculations, exclusions and uncertainty. For commercial/financial scenarios, expose inputs, units, time horizon and assumptions rather than presenting a forecast as achieved performance or promising returns. Legal/regulatory or investment-specific advice needs actual context and current authoritative sources; document writing is not certification.

## Adapt the requested artifact

Use the smallest outline that serves the reader. A reusable one-page brief can include:

1. Problem and intended user, grounded in observed/source-supported need.
2. Product and actual current capability, with a concrete supported use case.
3. Alternative and defensible difference, including limits and adoption burden.
4. Evidence so far, with source/date and the precise meaning of each number.
5. Business/distribution hypothesis, clearly labeled as a hypothesis where unproven.
6. Team and actual contributions using approved biography only.
7. Requested decision/help/resources and the real next milestone.
8. Material risks, dependencies and confidential details excluded from this audience.

For a pitch deck or detailed plan, expand only the requested sections and use the actual supplied template. Preserve source attribution, disclosure constraints and readable charts; no fabricated demo screenshot, growth curve or customer logo. A restricted internal document does not become approved for investor distribution because it is summarized. Do not copy raw financial/customer records to a provider merely to improve wording.

## Editorial acceptance and delivery

Each material claim is sourced, an explicit assumption, or a clearly marked missing fact. The reader should distinguish implemented product, observed customer evidence and planned roadmap. Reconcile calculations and terminology against the supplied data by inspection; no test/benchmark or account query is implied. Provide the complete requested draft with confirmation notes outside reusable copy. Save/share externally only within the actual request; exact recipients, final content, attachments and audience must fit action authorization before sending.

Expected example, not a pitch run: a Demir Bot brief may describe the existing 26 instruction skills and optional role definitions, but cannot claim measured time savings, autonomous reliability, revenue or customers without evidence. No-action case: a professor-email rewrite does not activate a venture assessment. Actual venture documents and contact remain pending the chosen brief and confirmed facts; this item installs no CRM, fundraising tool or sending access.
