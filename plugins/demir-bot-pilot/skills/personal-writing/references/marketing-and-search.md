# Evidence-based marketing and search visibility

Use for requested SEO, campaign planning, competitor comparison or analytics work on a named product/site. Resolve the actual audience, user problem, offer, public source/draft, allowed channels, geography and success decision. Use approved facts and existing product evidence; do not infer traction, ranking, conversion or account access from a website URL.

## Search visibility

Inspect only the selected accessible pages and source when an audit/change is requested. Consider whether intended public content is accessible, descriptive titles/headings, meaningful links, readable structure, duplicate/canonical content and image descriptions. Private pages must remain private; a search directive is not an access-control mechanism. Do not rewrite URLs, robots/canonical settings, sitemap or structured data without understanding the actual host and change scope. Use version-current search-engine guidance for consequential platform claims.

The [Google Search Central starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), reviewed 16 October 2026, supports useful audience-first content and understandable page/link structure. It does not promise inclusion or top ranking; changes can have variable effects and delay. Do not invent algorithm tricks, guaranteed keywords or a ranking deadline. The current reference is guidance, not an audit of Demir Bot's live site.

## Campaign and competitor deliverables

For a requested plan, map audience/problem, supported value proposition, content/source, chosen channel, desired action, owner and a realistic measurement window. Draft only the selected artifacts; no automatic calendar, recurring monitor, media purchase, lead collection, outreach or publication. A competitor comparison records dated primary evidence, actual feature/price constraints and uncertainty; public marketing assertions are not independent efficacy proof. Compare the existing/manual alternative as well as competing products.

For copy, adapt the approved source using content-repurposing.md and relevant channel tone. Preserve prototype/production distinctions, team credit, measured versus aspirational outcomes and meaningful limitations. Label unsupported claims for confirmation or omit them; do not pad source gaps with testimonials, users or impact numbers.

## Analytics and acceptance

Use supplied or specifically authorized account data with metric definitions, date/timezone, window, population and known paid/promotion effects. Distinguish impressions, clicks, meaningful actions and conversions; missing data is unavailable, not zero. Avoid causal claims from a small observational comparison. Installing tracking, changing consent/retention or sending analytics data requires its own concrete privacy/legal and operation scope. Do not add trackers as a routine SEO step.

Expected example, not a run: a requested Demir Bot landing-page draft explains its actually delivered skills, source/install limitations and clear intended action without claiming measured productivity gains. No-action case: a simple Grynd LinkedIn draft does not activate an SEO audit or advertising campaign. Real-site changes, analytics connections, publication and measured marketing effectiveness remain pending the selected site/accounts and specific actions.
